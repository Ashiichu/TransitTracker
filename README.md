TransitTracker

## TransitTracker is a platform that connects route taxi drivers and passengers in real time.  
It allows passengers to see nearby active route taxis and buses and enables drivers to identify potential passengers along their government-assigned routes.

Built during the Intellibus Hackathon.

## Problem
Passengers often struggle to find available route taxis/buses quickly, while drivers cannot easily identify where passengers are waiting along their routes.

## Solution
TransitTracker provides a real-time platform that improves visibility for both drivers and passengers, making route taxi/buses transportation more efficient and predictable. It also has the ability for passengers to make reservations with route taxis/buses

## Features

- Real-time tracking of active route taxis and buses
- Passengers can view nearby taxis/buses on available routes
- Drivers can see potential passengers along their assigned routes
- Route-based system aligned with government taxi routes

---

## How It Works

1. Drivers activate their route and share their location.
2. Passengers open the app to view nearby taxis.
3. Drivers can see passengers within their assigned route.
4. Both parties connect more efficiently for faster pickups.

## Purpose

TransitTaxi aims to improve the efficiency of route taxi transportation by providing visibility for both drivers and passengers, reducing wait times and improving route coordination.
